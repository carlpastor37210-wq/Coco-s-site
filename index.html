Our Story

Passion, quality, and a love for great coffee

Est. in Kaunas

A Place to Call Home

Coco's Café was born from a simple dream: to create a warm, welcoming space where the community could gather over exceptional coffee. Nestled in the heart of Kaunas, our café combines the charm of a traditional coffee house with modern sensibilities.

We source our beans from local roasters and work closely with farmers to ensure every cup is crafted with care. Our menu features seasonal specialties, artisan pastries, and wholesome dishes made from scratch daily.
